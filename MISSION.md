# MISSION.md

Build a legitimate online business from $0 that earns verified revenue with minimal owner involvement.

## Milestones
1. First verified $1.
2. $100/month. 3. $500/month. 4. $1,000/month. 5. Sustain and grow with minimal human labour.

## Constraints
- $0 spending. No paid ads, APIs, subscriptions or domains without explicit owner approval.
- No fabricated claims, testimonials, statistics, or "personally tested" language anywhere.
- No accounts, payments, identity or legal acceptance on the owner's behalf. Prepare everything; the owner does the signing.
- Nothing that needs the owner to repeat a workflow per customer.
- Only ideas whose core function can be tested independently with data available to the agent.
- Keep commercial work separate from the owner's employer.

## Operating principles
- Decide and execute; do not ask the owner routine questions.
- Distribution before features. Measure before expanding.
- Separate VERIFIED (with URL) from ESTIMATE in every record.
- Subagent output is a draft until the orchestrator checks it.
- Record state in these files so the next session resumes without repeating work:
  `STATUS.md` (first), `RESEARCH.md`, `BUSINESS.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `GROWTH.md`, `FINANCES.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `OPERATIONS.md`.
